About Bubble Shooter

Welcome to Bubble Shooter, a fun and colorful free online arcade game on PlayNova. Aim your bubble launcher, shoot colorful bubbles and match three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them.

Plan your shots carefully, use the walls to reach difficult places and clear the board before the bubbles reach the bottom. Challenge yourself to earn a high score!

Game Features

  • Colorful glossy bubble graphics.
  • Match three or more bubbles to pop them.
  • Mouse and mobile touch controls.
  • Score and best-score tracking.
  • Next bubble preview.
  • Sound effects and restart option.
  • Full screen gameplay.
  • Free browser game with no download required.

How to Play

1. Move your mouse or finger to aim the launcher.

2. Tap or click to shoot a bubble.

3. Match three or more bubbles of the same color.

4. Use wall rebounds to reach tricky areas.

5. Clear the board and try to beat your best score!
