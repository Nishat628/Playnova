About Neon Space Invaders

Neon Space Invaders is an exciting free online arcade shooter. Take control of a powerful spaceship, fire neon lasers and protect the galaxy from waves of alien invaders. Survive as long as possible and challenge your highest score!

Game Features

  • Fast-paced space shooting action.
  • Multiple waves of alien enemies.
  • Increasing difficulty as you progress.
  • Score and personal best tracking.
  • Neon graphics and particle effects.
  • Mobile-friendly touch controls.
  • Sound controls and full-screen mode.
  • Free to play directly in your browser.

How to Play

  • Use the left and right arrow keys to move.
  • Press SPACE to shoot on a computer.
  • On mobile, use the arrow buttons to move.
  • Hold FIRE to continuously shoot.
  • Destroy enemies before they reach your spaceship.
  • Avoid enemy lasers and protect your lives.
