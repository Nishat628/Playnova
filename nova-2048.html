About Nova 2048 & Strategy

Nova 2048 is a fast-paced number-merging puzzle game on PlayNova where your goal is to slide matching numbered tiles together to build higher values and reach the 2048 tile—or push even further for a top score. Every move shifts the board and brings in a new tile, so planning ahead helps keep the grid open.

Controls

On desktop, use your arrow keys or WASD to slide tiles across the grid. On mobile or tablet, simply swipe across the board in any direction to merge matching numbers smoothly.

Quick Tips for a Better Score

  • Keep your highest tile anchored in one corner, such as bottom-left or bottom-right.
  • Arrange tiles in a clean order along a row or column so merges happen naturally.
  • Avoid random swipes that scatter high tiles and fill up empty spaces too fast.
