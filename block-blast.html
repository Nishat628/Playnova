Block Blast — Grid Block Puzzle

Block Blast is a fast, tactical 8x8 block puzzle on PlayNova. Fit polyomino shapes onto the grid, complete full horizontal rows or vertical columns to blast blocks away, chain consecutive clears for combo multipliers, and keep space open as long as possible.

How to Play

  • Select one of the three block shapes in the bottom dock (or press keys 1, 2, or 3).
  • Move over the 8x8 grid to preview where the shape fits, then click or tap to place it.
  • Filling an entire row or column clears those cells and awards bonus points.
  • Clearing lines on consecutive placements builds your combo streak.

Settings & Controls

  • Fullscreen: Expand the game stage using the Fullscreen button in the top bar.
  • Settings: Toggle sound effects, placement preview outlines, or reduced neon glow; preferences save automatically in your browser.
