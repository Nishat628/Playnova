About Fruit Merge Mania

Welcome to Fruit Merge Mania, a colorful and exciting free online puzzle game on PlayNova. Drop fruits into the game board and combine matching fruits to create bigger ones. Every successful merge earns points and helps you build an impressive fruit collection.

Plan your moves carefully, avoid filling the board and try to reach the biggest fruit possible. Can you beat your highest score?

Game Features

  • 10 fruit evolution levels.
  • Colorful fruit graphics and smooth animations.
  • Score and best-score tracking.
  • Merge combos and bonus points.
  • Simple mouse and mobile touch controls.
  • Sound effects and restart option.
  • Free browser gameplay without downloads.

How to Play

1. Tap or click anywhere inside the game board to drop a fruit.

2. Match two fruits of the same type to merge them.

3. Create bigger fruits to earn more points.

4. Keep the board from filling up completely.

5. Try to achieve your highest score!
