Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 28, 2026

At PlayNova, we respect the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website, how it may be used, and what choices you have. Please read this policy carefully before using our website.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the PlayNova website and its browser-based games, pages and related features.

PlayNova provides online games and entertainment experiences that visitors can access through supported web browsers. You can browse available pages and play games without creating a personal account.

By using our website, you acknowledge that information may be handled as described in this policy. Where consent is legally required, we will seek it through an appropriate consent mechanism.

2. Information We May Collect

PlayNova does not require visitors to register an account to access its games. However, certain information may be collected automatically through website hosting, security tools, analytics services or other technologies.

  • Technical Information: Browser type, device type, operating system, screen size and general technical information.
  • Usage Information: Pages visited, time spent on pages, general interactions and referring website information, where analytics tools are enabled.
  • Approximate Location: General geographic information may be inferred from an IP address by hosting or third-party services.
  • Messages: If you contact us, we may receive the information you voluntarily include in your message, such as your email address and inquiry details.

We do not intentionally ask visitors to provide sensitive personal information through our games.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device. Similar technologies, including local storage, may also be used to support website features.

Depending on the services enabled on PlayNova, these technologies may be used for:

  • Remembering certain website preferences.
  • Saving game progress or high scores locally.
  • Understanding website performance and visitor activity.
  • Supporting advertising and measuring ad performance.
  • Improving website functionality and user experience.

You can manage or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings. Disabling certain storage features may affect some website or game functions.

4. Local Storage and Game Data

Some PlayNova games may use your browser's local storage to save information such as high scores, game progress, preferences or settings.

This information is generally stored on your own device rather than being submitted to a PlayNova account. Clearing your browser's site data may remove saved game progress or preferences.

The exact storage behavior may differ between individual games and browsers.

5. Google Analytics and Website Measurement

If Google Analytics or another analytics service is enabled on PlayNova, it may collect information about how visitors use the website.

This information may include pages viewed, interactions, approximate geographic information, device details, browser information and general usage patterns.

We use analytics information, where available, to understand website performance, identify technical issues and improve the experience for visitors.

Google's handling of information is subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy .

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

PlayNova may display advertisements through Google AdSense or other advertising partners if advertising services are enabled on the website.

Third-party advertising providers, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads, limit repeated advertisements, measure advertising performance and provide reporting.

Depending on applicable settings, user consent and eligibility, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as browser identifiers, cookie data, IP-related information and interactions with advertisements in accordance with their own policies and applicable laws.

For information about how Google uses data on partner websites, visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

You can learn about Google's advertising settings here: Google My Ad Center .

You can also review Google's information about advertising cookies here: Google Advertising Policies and Technologies .

7. Third-Party Services and External Links

PlayNova may use third-party services for website hosting, analytics, advertising, security or other technical functions.

Our website may also contain links to external websites. When you visit an external website, its own privacy policy and terms apply.

PlayNova does not control the privacy practices, content or security procedures of independent third-party websites and services.

8. How We Use Information

Information collected through our website or third-party services may be used for legitimate website-related purposes, including:

  • Operating and maintaining the website.
  • Improving games, navigation and website performance.
  • Understanding general visitor activity.
  • Responding to questions, suggestions and support requests.
  • Detecting technical problems, abuse or security issues.
  • Supporting advertising and measuring its performance where enabled.

We do not sell personal information directly to other companies as part of our website operations.

9. Children's Privacy

PlayNova is a general-audience gaming website. Our website is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information through our games. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of online services and review the privacy practices of any third-party services they use.

10. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of our website and protect information handled through our services.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information may be retained by hosting providers, analytics services, advertising partners or other third-party providers according to their own retention policies and applicable legal requirements.

11. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights relating to your personal information. These may include requesting access, correction or deletion of information, or objecting to certain processing.

  • You can manage cookies through your browser settings.
  • You can clear locally stored game data through browser settings.
  • You can review Google's advertising privacy and personalization settings.
  • You can contact us with privacy-related questions or requests.

Where required by applicable law, we will provide appropriate mechanisms for managing consent and privacy preferences.

12. International Visitors

Visitors may access PlayNova from different countries. Depending on the services used, information may be processed by hosting providers and third-party services in locations outside the visitor's country.

Such processing is subject to applicable laws and the privacy policies of the relevant service providers.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact us through our Contact page.

Visit the PlayNova Contact Page

We will review privacy-related inquiries and respond where appropriate.
