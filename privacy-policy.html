Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PlayNova handles information when you visit and play browser games on our website. We aim to keep gameplay simple, lightweight, and transparent.

2. Information We Collect & Browser Storage

You can browse PlayNova and play available games without creating an account. Standard web hosting or analytics tools may automatically receive basic technical details such as browser type, device screen size, referring pages, and approximate region.

  • Local Storage / Game Data: Certain games or preferences may store progress, high scores, or settings locally in your browser.
  • Direct Messages: If you contact us via email or our Contact page, we only use your message to respond to your inquiry.

3. Cookies, Analytics & Advertising

PlayNova may use cookies, local storage, or third-party services (such as web analytics or advertising networks) to measure site performance, improve features, or display relevant ads.

Third-party vendors and ad networks may use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy policies. You can manage or clear cookies and site data anytime through your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external websites, tools, or partner pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

5. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate oversight. If you believe personal information has been shared inadvertently, please reach out through our Contact page so we can address it promptly.

6. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as PlayNova evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

For questions about this policy, visit our Contact page.
