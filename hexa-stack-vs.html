Play Hexa Stack VS free in your browser on PlayNova. Place colorful stacked tiles onto the board, clear matching color groups for bonus points, outscore your opponent, and earn gold stars in Daily Challenges.

How to Play & Features

Select one of your three stacked color pieces at the bottom and tap an open tile slot on the grid. Matching colors merge and clear when stacks reach 4 or more layers, awarding extra points toward the target score.

Puzzle Tips

  • Keep open slots available so you always have room for larger stacks.
  • Match same-colored tops near neighboring slots to trigger higher combo points.
  • Check Daily Challenges regularly to earn gold stars and maintain your streak.
