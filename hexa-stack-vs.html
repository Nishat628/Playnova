Play Hexa Stack VS Online — Color Stack Merge Puzzle

Play Hexa Stack VS free in your browser on PlayNova. Stack colorful tiles up to 10 layers on empty or existing slots, pull matching tiles from up, down, left, and right neighbors, blast 10-tile stacks for big bonuses, and outscore your opponent.

How to Play & Features

Select one of your three stacks below and tap any slot where the stack fits within the 10-tile limit. Adjacent slots with the same top color send matching top tiles over to merge, and reaching 10 tiles in a slot blasts the stack for +100 bonus points.

Puzzle Tips

  • Look for gold-outlined slots—they indicate an immediate neighbor color merge.
  • Stack onto existing slots when total tiles stay 10 or fewer to build faster 10-tile blasts.
