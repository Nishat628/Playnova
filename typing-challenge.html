Play Typing Challenge Online — Free WPM & Accuracy Test

Play Typing Challenge online for free on PlayNova and test your words per minute (WPM) and typing accuracy in 30s or 60s rounds. Type the highlighted prompt text smoothly, track live speed and accuracy stats, and beat your personal best WPM score in your browser.

How to Play & Controls

Choose the 30s or 60s timer mode, tap or click the typing box, and begin typing the shown text—the countdown starts automatically on your first keystroke. Use Restart anytime for a fresh sentence round.

Typing Speed & Accuracy Tips

  • Focus on steady rhythm and accuracy first—fixing typos slows down your WPM more than typing slightly calmer.
  • Keep your eyes on the upcoming words and highlighted character so your fingers stay ahead.
  • Try 60s rounds for endurance and 30s rounds for quick speed bursts to push your best WPM higher.
