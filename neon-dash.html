About Neon Dash

Neon Dash is a fast-paced arcade runner created by PlayNova. Dodge dangerous obstacles, jump across the neon world and challenge yourself to achieve a new high score. The game gets faster as you progress, so stay alert and keep moving!

How to Play

  • Tap the screen or press the Space key to jump.
  • Avoid the glowing obstacles.
  • Survive longer to earn a higher score.
  • Try to beat your saved best score.
