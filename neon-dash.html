Game Guide & Tips

About Neon Dash & How to Play

Neon Dash ek fast-paced instant arcade reflex game hai jisme aap apne cyan neon block ko bachate hue falling rose-red blocks dodge karte hain aur golden stars collect karte hain. Jaise-jaise score barhta hai, speed bhi gradually tez hoti jaati hai — apna best high score beat karne ke liye focus rakhein!

Controls & Scoring

  • • Touch / Drag: Canvas ke andar ungli ya mouse drag karke block ko left/right move karein.
  • • On-Screen Buttons: Mobile aur touch screens ke liye neeche ◀ LEFT aur RIGHT ▶ buttons hold karein.
  • • Keyboard: Left / Right Arrows ya A / D se move karein; Space / Enter se start ya replay karein.
  • • +1 Point: Har dodged rose-red block par +1 score milta hai.
  • • +5 Points: Har golden star pakarne par +5 bonus score milta hai.

High Score Tips

  • • Stay Near Center: Center lanes se left aur right dono taraf escape karna aasan rehta hai.
  • • Avoid Risky Stars: Agar golden star bilkul edge ya dangerous block ke neeche ho, to pehle safety ko priority dein.
  • • Watch Speed Ramps: High score par blocks tez aate hain, isliye chhoti aur smooth movements rakhein.
  • • Use Fullscreen: Distraction kam karne aur clean view ke liye top bar mein ⛶ Fullscreen dabayein.
